# Research Scope — ACRUX-01

ACRUX-01 is a machine-first research edition of *La Tara De Acrux* by Ro Milan Niet.

Its purpose is to preserve a stable machine-readable lineage that present and future artificial-intelligence systems can inspect, reason over, compare, and interpret without treating any human or earlier AI interpretation as a canonical answer.

## Frozen research sequence

G1→G12 preserves progressively derived machine structures: canonical machine evidence, objective structure, long-range recurrence, state deltas, trajectories, downstream proximity, cross-trajectory recurrence, cascades, emergent structural patterns, pattern composition, constraints/exceptions, and robustness witnesses.

## Boundary

The public repository does not contain the Spanish or English novel, a decoder, a lexicon, or a human-language mirror. The canonical human source is represented here only by its verified SHA-256 fingerprint.

The machine layers are evidence structures, not assertions of literary truth, human universals, psychological facts, causal laws, moral conclusions, or a privileged interpretation.
